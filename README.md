# SYNR

### PC hardware. Open control.

SYNR is a PC hardware company focused on building high-performance components and peripherals with a strong emphasis on design, engineering, and user freedom.

We design the hardware.  
We build the ecosystem.  
We keep the software open.

---

## What is SYNR?

SYNR is developing a range of PC hardware including:

- Memory
- Case Fans
- AIO Cooling
- PC Cases
- Future PC components

Our products are designed to work together as a unified ecosystem while remaining open and accessible to users and developers.

---

## Open Ecosystem

We believe hardware shouldn't require proprietary software to be useful.

SYNR's hardware engineering and manufacturing designs remain proprietary, while our control ecosystem is designed to be open.

This includes:

- RGB control
- Hardware APIs
- Device communication
- Developer tools
- Integrations
- Documentation

Our goal is simple:

> **Your hardware. Your software. Your choice.**

---

## SYNR Engineering

Our hardware is developed with a focus on:

- Reliability
- Performance
- Thermal efficiency
- Industrial design
- Modularity
- Long-term support

We work with established OEM/ODM manufacturing partners while progressively bringing more engineering and product development in-house.
